# C negative patterns

1. **Portrait cutout glow** — a bright contour around hair/shoulders/legs reveals masking and feels synthetic.
2. **Night-to-studio drift** — walls, skin, or hair become too bright as if a key light was added.
3. **Hard practical light** — streetlights become sharp yellow patches instead of soft low-light sources.
4. **Uniform background blur** — every non-person pixel receives the same blur, destroying depth logic.
5. **Lookalike substitution** — inset/collage contains a newly generated “similar” person rather than the same person.
6. **Plastic skin** — excessive clarity reduction, denoise, frequency smoothing, or texture replacement removes pores and dimensionality.
7. **Face geometry drift** — eye/nose/jaw/lip proportions shift even though the task was only grading.
8. **Body proportion drift** — legs, waist, shoulders, hands, or stance are subtly reconstructed.
9. **Global cinematic wash** — teal/orange or warm-brown preset changes everything including skin, clothes, walls, and shadows.
10. **Fake bokeh** — lights/background blur shape does not follow scene depth or source optics.
11. **Over-design** — typography, collage, stickers, or frames become the subject and the person becomes decorative material.
12. **HDR portrait** — all shadows lifted, all skin bright, microcontrast exaggerated, night atmosphere lost.
