# C workflow — portrait-first edit

## User-package stage contract

In combined-user generation, the base stage is normal A for a scene or normal C for a portrait, followed by required strong B unless the current user explicitly says grading-only/no-design or requests a lower B strength. Keep the caller's contract throughout local routing. Base QC concerns the photographic master; it does not prohibit B page layout or certify the entire final deliverable. Preserve person/scene identity in B; do not repaint the person to fit a page. Standalone photographic workflows remain standalone.

## 1. Lock identity
Before any styling, treat face/body/hair/clothes/pose as protected source facts.

## 2. Stabilize the photo
Correct basic exposure and white balance without making the image brighter merely because it is a portrait.

## 3. Separate subject and environment
Use soft local masks/layers. Let the person gain attention through small tonal/contrast differences; let the environment recede through lower brightness, saturation, clarity, or depth only where justified.

## 4. Skin/face pass
When skin is visible, use local treatment. Preserve texture and natural asymmetry. Correct uneven color, not human identity.

## 5. Depth pass
Build near/mid/far layers. Avoid binary cutout blur. A wall next to the subject should not blur like a distant building.

## 6. Atmosphere pass
Night: soft practical lights, dim ambient exposure, realistic noise, restrained bloom, no hard rim light.
Day: preserve actual light direction and quality.

## 7. Color pass
Protect skin and clothing. Grade tonal ranges separately at low saturation.

## 8. Finish
Fine grain/softness only after structure is correct.

## 9. QC
The result should plausibly be achievable through careful Lightroom / Capture One / Camera Raw style local editing of the same photograph.
