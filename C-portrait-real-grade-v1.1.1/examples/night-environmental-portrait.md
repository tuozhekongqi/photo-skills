# Night environmental portrait — target behavior

## User-package stage contract

In combined-user generation, the base stage is normal A for a scene or normal C for a portrait, followed by required strong B unless the current user explicitly says grading-only/no-design or requests a lower B strength. Keep the caller's contract throughout local routing. Base QC concerns the photographic master; it does not prohibit B page layout or certify the entire final deliverable. Preserve person/scene identity in B; do not repaint the person to fit a page. Standalone photographic workflows remain standalone.

Input: full-body/back-view person walking or standing in a night street/campus environment.

Desired C-NIGHT treatment:
- keep the frame recognizably night;
- do not globally lift exposure;
- subtly lift subject midtones only if needed;
- darken or soften competing wall/road highlights;
- let distant practical lights become naturally softer than nearby surfaces;
- preserve some low-light noise/texture;
- keep warm lamps warm and distant blue/cool light cool when source-supported;
- no hard warm rim around hair or shoulders;
- no fake portrait-lens blur on adjacent wall/ground;
- fine grain only after the hierarchy works.

If followed by B:
- environmental inset is safe and often effective;
- person inset must be a crop from the same source/master or another confirmed same-person photo;
- if identity continuity is uncertain, do not include a person inset.
