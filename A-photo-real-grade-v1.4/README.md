# A — Photo Real Grade v1.4 (lightweight)

This is the **real-photography finishing** skill. It is physically separated from Photo Zine and optimized to stay small enough to share and use.

## What it may change
Exposure, white balance, highlight/shadow placement, tonal curve, local contrast, believable color separation, restrained clarity/sharpening, and source-supported light emphasis.

## What it must not change by default
Composition, architecture, text/signage, object identity, scene content, weather, time of day, or photographic identity. People/clutter are changed only when explicitly requested, and people are judged semantically rather than by distance alone.

## Image library
- `reference-library/positive/` — compact user-approved references only.
- `reference-library/cautionary/` — only a few archetypal failures.

There is **no secondary image library** and **no teaching/research image archive** in the distributable skill. Their useful lessons have been distilled into text under `references/`.
