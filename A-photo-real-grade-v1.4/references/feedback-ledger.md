# A — Feedback Ledger

This ledger stores user feedback **as scoped text first**. Only explicit strong positives are candidates for the compact positive image library. “还行 / 一般 / negative” lessons remain textual.

## Stable baseline
- The user is usually already satisfied with the original photograph.
- A should therefore default to **near-original / minimum necessary intervention**, not “make it more cinematic at all costs.”
- Stronger cinematic shaping is conditional: only when the source already contains the relevant light/contrast and the current image clearly benefits.
- A valid result may look only subtly different from the source.

## Strong / positive directions
- Realism remains the hard boundary. Cinematic value, when appropriate, comes from believable density, local light hierarchy, color separation, material texture, and composition — not invented sun, fake rays, sky replacement, or scene repainting.
- Overcast scenes may still feel cinematic. Keep the weather; use masonry separation, red/green relationships, shadow density, and spatial depth rather than converting the frame to blue-sky or sunset.
- People are handled **semantically**, not by distance alone. Back-facing or side-facing figures near an edge may remain if they add scale, quietness, direction, or lived-in atmosphere.
- Keep some middle/far-background people in public spaces when they help scale and reality. Do not turn real locations into sterile “AI sample roads.”
- The fuller tree-lined avenue composition was preferred over a canopy-dominant crop: preserve the spatial story when the path/avenue is the subject.
- The close-up tree-bark/insect result was approved: selective focus, real surface texture, shallow depth of field, and restrained background enhancement can work when source-supported.
- The red-wall/tree/sky photograph was rated “不错”: separation and clarity worked because the source remained photographic; the lesson is not “make sky bluer.”
- The red-bench park result was rated “不错”: the successful part was **restraint** — original composition preserved, red bench remained the anchor, natural light was only lightly organized, and the scene did not look rebuilt.

## “还行” lessons — keep as text
- **Along-wall depth view:** near blurred wall + distant corridor perspective was useful; far people supplied scale. Weakness: sky and saturation were too strong. Learn the depth logic, not the blue-sky look.
- **Symmetrical palace gate:** central symmetry, red wall, stone bridge, and roof layering worked. Weakness: sky became too dramatic. Learn architectural hierarchy, not sky replacement.

## “一般 / 过头” lessons
- **Long trench / red-wall perspective:** too much blue-sky conversion, green saturation, and HDR/travel-promo polish.
- **Long roof / exterior wall:** cloud structure and highlight drama became the visual subject, creating a repainted feeling.
- **Golden tree avenue over-edit:** local warm light was expanded across the entire canopy, greens shifted yellow, the vanishing-point glow became synthetic, and the scene looked re-rendered. Correction: preserve natural green, keep warmth local, reduce glow, and accept the original tonal balance.
- “过头” normally means lower the specific intensity dimension; it does not mean A as a whole is wrong.

## Hard negative patterns
- Full-frame regeneration when the task is only photographic finishing or local cleanup.
- Replacing real weather with blue sky, golden hour, dramatic cloud structure, or artificial sunlight.
- Globally bright foliage, uniformly warm light, excessive clarity/dehaze, over-detailed clouds, or polished surfaces that make the frame feel generated.
- Deleting every person simply because the nominal subject is not a person.
- “AI味重” commonly combines reconstruction + over-clean texture + invented light + loss of small real irregularities.

## Current target
A should feel like **the same photograph, carefully finished only where needed**. If the source is already good, the best A may be almost unchanged. “Photographic truth + restraint” outranks visible editing strength.
