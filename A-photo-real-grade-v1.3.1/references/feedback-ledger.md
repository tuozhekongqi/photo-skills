# A — Feedback Ledger

This ledger stores user feedback as **text first**. Only explicit strong positives are kept as images. “还行 / 一般 / 不好” feedback is normally distilled here and the images are discarded from the runtime package.

## Strong / positive directions

- Realism remains the hard boundary, but the preferred A grade is **not timid**: stronger tonal hierarchy, deeper but textured shadows, more local contrast, stronger warm/cool separation, and more atmosphere are welcome when source-supported.
- Cinematic feeling must come from believable density, local light hierarchy, color separation, material texture, and composition — not invented sun, fake rays, sky replacement, or scene repainting.
- Overcast scenes may still feel cinematic. Keep the weather; use grey-brick separation, red/green relationships, shadow density, and spatial depth rather than converting the frame to blue-sky or sunset.
- For non-human subjects, people are handled **semantically**. Back-facing or side-facing walkers near an edge may remain even when relatively close if they add scale, quietness, direction, or lived-in atmosphere.
- Remove people mainly when they are large, front-facing, central, visually loud, or clearly competing with the actual subject.
- Keep some middle/far-background people in public spaces. A real location should not become a sterile “AI sample road.”
- The fuller tree-lined avenue composition was preferred over a canopy-dominant crop: preserve the full spatial story when the path/avenue is the subject.
- The close-up tree-bark/insect result was approved: selective focus, real surface texture, shallow depth of field, and restrained background enhancement are valid cues.
- The recent red-wall/tree/sky photograph was rated “不错” and is now a positive image reference. Useful cue: stronger photographic clarity and separation can work when the source structure remains recognizable and the overall result still reads as photography.

## “还行” lessons — keep as text, not images

- **Along-wall depth view:** the near blurred wall + distant corridor perspective was useful; far people appropriately supplied scale. Weakness: sky and saturation were still too strong. Learn the depth logic, not the blue-sky look.
- **Symmetrical palace gate:** central symmetry, red wall, stone bridge, and roof layering worked. Weakness: the sky became too dramatic. Learn architectural hierarchy, not sky replacement.
- “还行” means there is something useful to extract, but the whole image is **not** a reference target.

## “一般” lessons — diagnosis only, no image retention

- **Long trench / red-wall perspective:** too much blue-sky conversion, green saturation, and HDR/travel-promo polish. Better: preserve real weather and use masonry, red walls, perspective, and muted greens for cinematic separation.
- **Long roof / exterior wall:** cloud structure and highlight drama became the visual subject, producing a repainted/AI feeling. Better: let masonry, roof ridge, red columns, and tree line carry the image.
- “一般” signals a direction that may contain useful pieces but needs substantial correction; do not keep the image.

## Hard negative patterns

- Full-frame regeneration when the task is only local cleanup.
- Replacing overcast weather with blue sky, golden hour, dramatic cloud structure, or artificial sunlight.
- Deleting every person because the subject is not a person.
- Globally bright green foliage, uniformly warm light, excessive local clarity, over-detailed clouds, and polished surfaces that make the frame feel generated.
- “AI味重” often combines four failures: reconstruction + over-clean texture + invented light + loss of small real irregularities.

## Current default target

A should feel like a strong photographer/colorist finished the **same frame**: visibly cinematic, materially believable, and still plausible as careful photographic post-processing.
