# Negative patterns to avoid

1. **Invented light**: new sunbeams, halos, rim-light rings, reflections, or sunset colors unsupported by the source.
2. **AI reconstruction**: foliage, masonry, clouds, water, faces, boats, railings, or signs becoming newly drawn rather than edited.
3. **Global cinematic preset**: pushing every scene into orange/teal, teal shadows + orange highlights, or uniform warm gold.
4. **HDR violence**: lifted shadows everywhere + compressed highlights + aggressive microcontrast, causing flat depth and synthetic texture.
5. **Crushed blacks without purpose**: loss of material detail simply to look "cinematic".
6. **Clipped glow**: bright zones blooming into featureless white/yellow.
7. **Fake film texture**: grain, dust, scratches, gate weave, vignette, or halation added automatically.
8. **Over-sharpened edges**: halos around bridge rails, tree leaves, architecture, people, or horizon lines.
9. **Plastic cleanup**: removing natural irregularities, noise, surface wear, or facial texture until the image feels generated.
10. **Over-designed realism**: making a normal park path or bridge scene dramatically different from what was actually photographed.


## Storage note
Most negative examples are text-only. The runtime package keeps only a few archetypal cautionary images: invented/repainted light, over-clean empty public space, and full-scene repainting.
