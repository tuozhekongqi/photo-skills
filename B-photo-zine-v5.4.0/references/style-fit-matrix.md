# Style Fit Matrix v5.0

## Strength and photographic-restraint scope

Read `delivery-contract.md` first. Default strong B is independent of photographic T/PRESERVE. Quiet/photo-led means less repainting/clutter, not weaker page design; historical taste cannot lower the current contract.

Use this as the anti-template companion to the 12 style families. Each family includes strong signals, contraindications, and a fallback.

| Family | Strong signals | Do **not** use when | Typical photographic transformability | Typical fidelity | Safer fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Travel Ticket | iconic destination, souvenir logic, moderate whitespace, clear motif set | scene is documentary, very intimate, visually dense, or the ticket would feel gimmicky | T1-T2 | PRESERVE/HYBRID | J or E |
| B Watercolor Travel Editorial | soft landscape, water, garden, atmospheric architecture, gentle palette | hard industrial geometry, strong night neon, documentary people, texture must stay literal | T2 | HYBRID | G or E |
| C Minimal Collage Poster | strong geometry, silhouette, architecture, road, graphic crop, whitespace | composition depends on subtle atmosphere or human intimacy; source already perfect and needs no intervention | T2-T3 | HYBRID | J or G |
| D Second World | one actionable structure, clear edge/path/cable/reflection/bridge, concept reads instantly | no actionable structure; concept needs explanation; source is already complete; forced tiny-people gimmick | T3 | HYBRID | C or G |

## Family-specific rejection tests

### A — Travel Ticket
Reject if:
- more than 30% of the visual idea comes from invented ticket decoration;
- the user would remember the graphic ticket more than the actual place;
- the photo is too dense to leave a clean lower field.

### B — Watercolor
Reject if:
- the watercolor removes the scene's architectural precision;
- it turns a documentary moment into generic travel illustration;
- greens/blues become generic AI watercolor rather than source-derived.

### C — Minimal Collage
Reject if:
- collage exists only to look designed;
- the source has no strong geometry to justify cropping or graphic planes;
- multiple scraps compete with the hero image.

### D — Second World
Reject if:
- the concept can be applied to almost any photo;
- line figures are posing rather than doing useful work;
- the source cut shape does not participate in the concept;
- the viewer needs a paragraph to understand the joke/idea.

### E — Soft Analog
Reject if:
- film effects become more visible than the photograph;
- fake dust / leak / sepia is doing the emotional work;
- the photo needs formal graphic structure rather than diary intimacy.

### F — Museum Catalogue
Reject if:
- fake metadata pretends to be official;
- the scene lacks an object/study quality;
- the result becomes luxury-brand advertising.

### G — Light & Negative Space
Reject if:
- empty space is created by destructive cropping rather than naturally present;
- important detail becomes too small;
- the image is busy and cannot breathe.

### H — Cinematic Still
Reject if:
- the treatment relies on teal-orange cliché, fake lens flare, heavy letterbox bars;
- there is no implied narrative or atmosphere to support cinematic framing.

### I — Modern Chinese Minimal
Reject if:
- the design reduces Chinese visual language to vermilion + gold clichés;
- the scene's source color/material logic is ignored;
- typography turns into tourism branding.

### J — Editorial Magazine
Reject if:
- copy blocks cover the best visual region;
- the headline is more important than the photo;
- page furniture is added without editorial function.

### K — Monochrome Documentary
Reject if:
- removing color destroys the photo's core meaning;
- fake grit or extreme contrast creates false drama;
- the image is decorative rather than observational.

### L — Detail Card
Reject if:
- the crop could come from anywhere and no longer carries place identity;
- the detail is not visually strong enough to justify isolation;
- too much decorative card chrome is added.
