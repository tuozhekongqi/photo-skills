# Decision Counterexamples v5.0

## Strength and photographic-restraint scope

Read `delivery-contract.md` first. Default strong B is independent of photographic T/PRESERVE. Quiet/photo-led means less repainting/clutter, not weaker page design; historical taste cannot lower the current contract.

These examples train the selector to avoid shallow subject-to-style matching.

---

## 1. Great Wall + vast sky
**Bad route:** `Great Wall -> Modern Chinese Minimal` automatically.

**Why bad:** The image may be about sky, ridge rhythm, or atmosphere rather than heritage styling.

**Better decision:**
- if negative space + cloud are strongest -> G;
- if travel movement / dramatic depth is strongest -> H;
- use I only when wall material, roof rhythm, or cultural geometry is actually dominant.

---

## 2. Cable car line across a valley
**Bad route:** `mountain -> watercolor`.

**Why bad:** The cable line may be the image's strongest actionable geometry.

**Better decision:** D if the line supports a natural concept; H if movement / suspended travel is more important.

---

## 3. Quiet lake with tiny pagoda
**Bad route:** `pagoda -> heritage / museum`.

**Why bad:** The pagoda may function only as a distant punctuation mark. The real subject may be empty water and atmosphere.

**Better decision:** G first; B second if painterly softness helps.

---

## 4. Strong red lantern in a gray hutong
**Bad route:** `hutong -> monochrome documentary`.

**Why bad:** Removing color destroys the red focal point.

**Better decision:** E or J, preserving red; D only if alley geometry supports a real second-world idea.

---

## 5. Dense city flyover at blue hour
**Bad route:** `city -> minimal collage` by default.

**Why bad:** The photograph may already have strong movement, lights, and atmosphere.

**Better decision:** H or E if the image is emotionally complete; C only if geometry clearly benefits from graphic simplification.

---

## 6. Dragon ceiling medallion
**Bad route:** `Chinese detail -> Modern Chinese Minimal` automatically.

**Why bad:** The radial motif may be better treated as an object/specimen.

**Better decision:** L if one detail is the hero; F if the full ceiling should be studied; I only if spatial cultural context matters.

---

## 7. Beautiful sunset over water
**Bad route:** `water -> watercolor`.

**Why bad:** Repainting may destroy the actual light.

**Better decision:** T0 + PRESERVE, G or H photographic handling, plus strong B page construction and second functional relation. Preserve the photo rendering; do not omit the page design.

---

## 8. Ordinary candid street moment
**Bad route:** `travel -> ticket`.

**Why bad:** Souvenir framing can make a lived moment feel staged.

**Better decision:** E or K depending whether color or structure matters more.

---

## 9. Symmetrical palace facade
**Bad route:** `architecture -> cinematic`.

**Why bad:** Cinematic grading may add drama that the image does not need.

**Better decision:** F, I, or J based on text tolerance and cultural context.

---

## 10. Single old tree with sculptural trunk
**Bad route:** `garden -> watercolor`.

**Why bad:** The trunk may be an archival object-like subject.

**Better decision:** F or G; B only if foliage and atmosphere matter more than form.

---

## 11. Rainy street with colored umbrellas
**Bad route:** `documentary -> monochrome`.

**Why bad:** Color may be the emotional rhythm.

**Better decision:** E or H.

---

## 12. Very graphic building against blank sky
**Bad route:** `architecture -> museum catalogue` automatically.

**Why bad:** The image may already behave like a poster.

**Better decision:** C or J; F only if study/archival framing adds meaning.

---

## 13. Reflection perfectly mirrors a bridge
**Bad route:** `bridge -> architectural study`.

**Why bad:** The true behavior may be duality / reflection, not construction.

**Better decision:** G if quiet; D if the reflection supports an immediate actionable second-world concept.

---

## 14. Busy market scene
**Bad route:** `travel -> watercolor magazine`.

**Why bad:** Painterly simplification may erase documentary energy.

**Better decision:** E, K, or J.

---

## 15. Textured stone dragon relief
**Bad route:** `dragon -> surreal pop`.

**Why bad:** Scale tricks may cheapen a strong historical surface.

**Better decision:** L, F, or Linocut Heritage as a controlled DISTILL treatment.

---

## 16. Foggy mountain ridge
**Bad route:** `mountain -> cinematic` automatically.

**Why bad:** Strong grading can overpower the natural silence.

**Better decision:** G; H only if there is travel movement or narrative timing.

---

## 17. Long road with strong vanishing point
**Bad route:** `road -> city editorial`.

**Why bad:** The road may be a conceptual or cinematic device rather than an urban subject.

**Better decision:** D if actionable; H if narrative; C if geometry is purely graphic.

---

## 18. Already-perfect editorial-looking photo
**Bad route:** force any visible design system because the skill is expected to “do something.”

**Why bad:** Styling can reduce quality.

**Better decision:** T0 + PRESERVE + strong B Clean Editorial / E / G / H page construction. Repaint less while maintaining the locked strong-design deliverable.
