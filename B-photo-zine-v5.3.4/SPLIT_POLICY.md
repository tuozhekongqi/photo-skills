# Split / combined-package policy — Skill B

This folder is the **current Photo Zine / editorial redesign runtime**. It has evolved beyond the original V5.3 rules.

The **original V5.3 golden backup** is preserved separately at the combined-package top level under `golden-backup/` and must remain byte-for-byte immutable. It is rollback material only, not live runtime guidance.

Under the combined-package default routing, B remains active for every normal photo task: **A+B** without a clear portrait/person subject and **A+B+C** with one. Visible editorial/page design is added only when it genuinely helps: magazine page, travel note, souvenir card, restrained collage, paper/material language, typography, Second World, or related visual design.

Simple “只调光影 / 只调色 / 保持原照片真实 / 只轻修” requests do **not** disable B. The route remains A+B for a non-portrait image, or A+B+C for a portrait, unless the user explicitly changes the module combination. A performs the photographic correction; B may be extremely restrained or near-no-op when visible editorial treatment would not help. A+B can remain single-source and does not imply collage.

**Module activation and visible intervention are separate decisions.**

A and B keep separate positive image libraries. Shared routing and feedback rules live at the combined-package top level.
