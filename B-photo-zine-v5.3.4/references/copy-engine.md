# Copy Engine v5.3

Text is secondary to the photograph. Copy should sound derived from the actual scene, not pulled from a generic quote bank.

---

## 1. Copy inputs

Use only information visible in the source or explicitly supplied by the user:
- place / landmark if known;
- weather / light;
- movement;
- material / texture;
- dominant visual behavior;
- emotional mode;
- journey context.

Never fabricate official names, dates, routes, historical facts, ticket numbers, or museum metadata.

---

## 2. Copy modes

### C0 — No text
Use when the image is visually complete or text tolerance is 0.

### C1 — Handwritten aside
- 5-12 English words;
- observational;
- quiet;
- slightly personal;
- not inspirational.

Examples of tone, not reusable stock lines:
- `The ridge kept going long after I stopped.`
- `Clouds made the wall feel almost weightless.`

### C2 — Editorial title + small line
- title: 1-3 words;
- support line: 4-10 words;
- useful for J, C, I, B.

### C3 — Archival label
- short title;
- one restrained metadata-like line;
- useful for F, L;
- do not invent official catalogue data.

### C4 — Ticket microcopy
- one destination-like title;
- one short mood / journey line;
- tiny decorative microtype may be abstract rather than factual.

---

## 3. Tone rules

Prefer:
- concrete nouns;
- observed actions;
- restrained emotion;
- subtle humor or wonder when natural.

Avoid:
- motivational slogans;
- life-advice language;
- generic `collecting moments`, `wander often`, `escape`, `same... different...` formulas repeated across a set;
- pseudo-profound abstraction detached from the image.

---

## 4. Placement rules

Placement is driven by visual space, not a fixed corner.

Do not cover:
- faces;
- landmark silhouettes;
- key horizon lines;
- primary movement paths;
- high-detail ornament.

Text may be placed:
- in natural sky / water / wall negative space;
- on paper field;
- in a margin;
- alongside an axis or crop edge.

---

## 5. Batch copy diversity

Track:
- title vocabulary;
- sentence structure;
- punctuation style;
- line length;
- placement;
- copy mode.

In a 6+ image set, include at least one no-text or near-no-text image when visually appropriate.

---

## 6. Copy quality gate

Reject copy if:
- it could fit any travel photo;
- it repeats wording from a prior output;
- it states facts not visible / known;
- it competes with the photo;
- it sounds like an ad, slogan, or inspirational poster.
