# B — Feedback Ledger

B stores feedback **text first**. Only explicit strong positives enter the compact positive image library. “还行 / 一般” images are not retained; their useful and weak traits become scoped text rules.

## Stable baseline
- B is a **design layer around the photograph**, not an excuse to repaint it.
- B can be a single-source editorial page. B does not imply collage.
- A+B can also be single-source: A/A-lite protects the photo, then B adds restrained design.
- Repetition is acceptable when multiple photographs genuinely support the same treatment. Do not force variety merely to look creative.

## Strong / positive directions
- Photo-first hierarchy: the source remains the emotional center; type, paper, collage, and annotations frame it rather than replace it.
- The approved tree-detail **Small Things** treatment validated a restrained editorial direction: one dominant source image, warm off-white paper, torn edge, elegant serif, short handwritten line, and only a small supporting visual element.
- **Red Wall, Soft Sky** validates large serif type and calm paper framing when the source offers real negative space. Learn hierarchy and quiet rhythm; do not make warm sky or torn paper mandatory.
- **Beijing / City Notes** validates a city-note editorial treatment where a strong architecture image stays dominant, text occupies real negative space, and the page feels quiet rather than sticker-heavy.
- **Wall and Heaven** validates a heritage layout where the photograph occupies the upper visual field, the title sits below rather than over the architecture, and supporting text stays sparse.
- The successful same-subject collage pattern is **one clear hero image + one small complementary view**. The small image must not cover the hero's key subject.
- Large serif typography is an option when the source has room for it, not a house template.

## Partial / “还行” lessons
- **Quiet Gate:** symmetrical architecture + title avoiding the central subject worked. Weakness: template repetition and weather warming.
- **Walls of Silence:** large building mass + title/note balance worked. Weakness: repeated paper/serif/warm-sky formula.
- Extract the specific layout principle; do not upgrade the whole treatment.

## “一般 / 不行” lessons
- **Corridor of Time:** repeated `large serif + off-white paper + torn edge + handwritten sentence + warm sky` ignored the source's actual long perspective, grey masonry, red wall, and trench geometry.
- Full-frame watercolor repainting was rejected. If watercolor is used, keep the photograph primary and apply it locally/partially unless the user explicitly asks for illustration.
- Filmstrip/contact-sheet treatment was rejected for this project; do not recreate the same signature under another name.
- Multi-photo dumps / grid collages were rejected. Collage must be source-related, small-scale, and compositionally justified.

## Cautionary patterns
- Do not reuse the same serif/torn-paper/warm-sky page across unrelated sources.
- Do not repaint weather or architecture just to fit a prettier editorial template.
- Do not let an auxiliary crop/panel cover the main subject.
- Do not interpret “try more styles” as “every batch must contain an experiment.”
- Do not interpret “no dumb collage” as “disable B.” Fix the collage decision, not the whole design system.
- If the photo already carries the image, B should add only the minimum graphic layer needed.

## Current target
B should feel like an art director built a page **around this specific photograph**. The photograph remains primary; design intensity is conditional; successful ideas are reusable principles, not mandatory templates.
